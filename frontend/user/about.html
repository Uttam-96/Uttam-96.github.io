About Me

Hello, I’m Deepak Parajuli — a teacher, writer, and lawyer based in Pokhara. I currently teach at P.N. Campus, where I guide students in political science and related subjects. Alongside teaching, I continue to write and share ideas, blending classroom knowledge with practical insights.

About This Platform

This platform is built with one simple motto:
“Learn Smarter, Anytime, Anywhere.”

Here, you’ll find exclusive notes, study materials, and subject-wise PDFs designed to support your learning journey beyond the classroom. Each subject is available for just NRS 100 with lifetime access, including free updates.

Who Is This For?

This platform is primarily designed for college students who want to strengthen their understanding of political science and related subjects.

My Vision

My goal is to make education more accessible, affordable, and practical. By combining my experience as a teacher and a lawyer, I aim to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world application—helping students not just pass exams, but also think critically and apply knowledge in their careers.
